About Me

I’m a third-year Computer Science and Engineering student at Yuan Ze University. I enjoy learning new skills and taking on challenges, especially in programming. My main languages are C++, C#, and Python, and I’ve used Python for data analysis and modeling projects. I also hold GEPT Intermediate (listening, speaking, reading, and writing) and TOEIC 670 certifications.

Academically, I ranked 3nd out of 144 students in my department and received the Academic Silver Award in both 2023 and 2024. I advanced to the final round of the 30th Information Application Service Innovation Competition and co-authored a paper applying Electronic Design Automation (EDA) to the field of music, reflecting my interest in combining computer science with other fields.

In 2025, I worked as a short-term research assistant at Academia Sinica, where I developed code to analyze and classify seismic waveform signals as part of a larger earthquake research project. This gave me a glimpse into real research and sparked my interest in interdisciplinary applications of computer science.

Outside of academics, I’ve gained teamwork and communication experience through high school debate and university dormitory activities. I’m always excited to explore new topics and expand my skills, especially across different fields.
